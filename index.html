Privacy policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026 · Applies to Pageroll – Full Page Screenshot & Editor, version 1.0.0 and later

Summary

Pageroll works entirely on your device. It doesn't collect, transmit, sell or share your data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it makes no network requests. The screenshots you take and the details of the pages you capture are stored only in your browser, on your computer, until you delete them.

  • No account
  • No uploads
  • No analytics
  • No ads
  • No remote code
  • Works offline

1.What Pageroll handles, and why

Pageroll handles data only when you ask it to take a capture, and only to provide its single purpose: capturing, editing and saving screenshots of the web page you are viewing.

DataWhenWhy
Page content as pixels: what the page shows on screen, including any text or personal information visible on it When you start a capture (full page, visible area or selected area) To build the screenshot image. During a full-page capture Pageroll's own script scrolls the page, loads its images and temporarily hides repeated sticky headers, then restores the page exactly as it was.
Page title and address (URL) of the captured pageSaved with each capture To show and search your captures in the library, to suggest a file name, and for the "Open original page" command in the editor.
Capture date and time, image size, capture typeSaved with each capture To sort and filter the library and show file sizes.
Your annotations (shapes, text, step numbers, redactions, crop)While you edit So you can reopen a capture and keep editing it.
Your settings (for example theme, language, image format, file name template, library limits)When you change them To remember your preferences.

Pageroll does not read form fields, passwords, cookies, browsing history, or any page you haven't asked it to capture. Mouse and keyboard input is used only live, to draw a selection rectangle or to cancel a capture with Esc. It is never recorded.

2.Where your data is stored

  • Only on your device, in your browser profile. Captures, thumbnails, annotations and page details are kept in the extension's own IndexedDB database. Settings are kept in chrome.storage.local. While a capture is running, its progress is kept briefly in chrome.storage.session, which lives in memory and is cleared when the browser closes.
  • Nothing is uploaded, synced or backed up by Pageroll. Pageroll doesn't use chrome.storage.sync.
  • Incognito: captures of incognito windows are never kept in the library. Such a capture exists only while its editor tab is open and is deleted automatically when you close that tab. The same applies to any capture you take while "Save captures to the library" is turned off.

3.What Pageroll does not do

  • No servers, no network requests and no remote code: everything Pageroll runs is inside the extension package.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising, cookies or third-party software development kits.
  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No sale, transfer or sharing of your data with anyone, and no human access to it.
  • No use of your data for advertising, creditworthiness, lending or any purpose unrelated to taking and editing screenshots.

4.When images leave Pageroll

Only when you do it:

  • Download / Export: the PNG, JPEG or PDF file you create is saved to your Downloads folder (or where you choose with "Save as…"). PDF files contain only the flattened image; Pageroll doesn't write the page title or address into the file's metadata.
  • Copy: the image is placed on your system clipboard. Pageroll never reads the clipboard.

From then on, the file is under your control. Redactions (solid or pixelated) are flattened into the exported pixels, so the hidden content can't be recovered from the file.

5.Retention and deletion

  • Captures stay in the library until you delete them, or until the automatic clean-up removes them. By default Pageroll keeps the latest 100 captures. In Options › Library you can choose how many to keep (or "Unlimited") and delete captures older than 7, 30, 90 or 365 days.
  • You can delete one capture, several, or all of them at any time, in the library or in Options › Library › "Delete all captures". Deleted captures are removed from storage right away (after a few seconds in which you can undo).
  • Uninstalling Pageroll removes all of its data (library and settings) from your browser.
  • Because the library uses Chrome's "unlimited storage" for extensions, clearing your browsing data may not remove it. Use "Delete all" in the library instead.

6.Permissions and why Pageroll needs them

PermissionWhat it is used for
activeTabGives temporary access to the tab you're on, only after you click the Pageroll icon or a Pageroll menu item, or press a Pageroll shortcut. Pageroll can't access any website until you ask for a capture, and access ends when you leave the page.
scriptingRuns Pageroll's own bundled capture script on that tab to measure and scroll the page, load lazy images, hide repeated sticky headers and draw the area-selection overlay, then put everything back.
storageSaves your settings on this device.
unlimitedStorageLets the local library hold large captures without hitting the storage quota or being evicted by Chrome.
downloadsSaves the files you export, with your file name and folder, and opens the "Save as" dialog if you turned it on. Chrome describes this permission as "Manage your downloads". Pageroll doesn't read, open or change your other downloads.
contextMenusAdds Pageroll's capture commands to the right-click menu and to the toolbar icon's menu.

Pageroll requests no access to websites in advance (no host permissions) and doesn't use the tabs, history, cookies, clipboardRead or webRequest permissions.

7.Security

Your captures are protected by your operating-system account and your browser profile, like the rest of your browser data. Screenshots can contain sensitive information that was visible on the screen: store and share them with care, and use redaction before sharing.

8.Children

Pageroll is a general-purpose tool. It isn't directed at children, and it doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

9.Chrome Web Store User Data Policy and Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Pageroll uses the data it handles only to provide its single purpose described above, keeps it on your device, and never sells it, transfers it or uses it for any other purpose.

10.Changes to this policy

If Pageroll's data practices change, this page will be updated and its effective date changed before the new version of the extension is published. Any change in how data is handled will also be shown to you inside the extension before it takes effect.
