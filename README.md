# Pageroll – Full Page Screenshot & Editor

Pageroll is a Chrome extension that captures entire web pages (or the visible area, or a selection),
lets you annotate and redact them, and exports PNG, JPEG or PDF. Everything happens locally in your
browser: no account, no uploads, no tracking.

- **Privacy policy:** https://enikqi.github.io/pageroll/
- **Support:** [open an issue](https://github.com/enikqi/pageroll/issues) — describe what happened, the page
  you were capturing (if you can share it) and your Chrome version.
